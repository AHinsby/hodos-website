# HODOS project rules

- Treat this repository as the source of truth for the HODOS website.
- Preserve the Danish language and the HODOS positioning: practical skills, safety, and adventure for families and adults.
- Do not invent certifications, instructors, testimonials, course dates, prices, or safety claims.
- Keep the supplied concept intact unless a requested change has a clear user-facing purpose.
- Keep credentials, keys, tracking identifiers, and production configuration out of Git.
- Create focused commits and validate HTML, CSS, and JavaScript before pushing.
- Do not deploy or connect a live domain without explicit user approval.
