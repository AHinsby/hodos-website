# UdeKlar – billedretning og promptpakke

Disse prompts er til midlertidige UdeKlar-visuals. De skal bruges som redaktionelle stemningsbilleder, ikke som dokumentation for rigtige UdeKlar-hold, konkrete lokationer eller allerede afholdte kurser.

## Fælles stilregel

- Nordisk, rolig, lys og taktil redaktionel stil
- Dansk/skandinavisk skov, hede, kyst eller åbent landskab
- Dæmpet skovgrøn, mosgrøn, sten, varm offwhite og naturligt morgen- eller eftermiddagslys
- Ingen logoer, tekst, vandmærker, badges eller militær æstetik
- Ingen ekstreme bjergtoppe, overlevelsesdrama eller farlige situationer
- Ingen genkendelige ansigter eller personer, der kan ligne virkelige UdeKlar-deltagere
- Landskab, hænder, rygfigurer eller små grupper set på afstand er bedst

## 1. Forside – læring i naturen

**Format:** 4:5, stående

> Nordic editorial outdoor image for a Danish skills and adventure school. A calm Scandinavian forest edge in soft early autumn light, a simple paper map and compass resting on a weathered wooden bench in the foreground, distant unidentifiable family silhouettes walking on a safe wide path, natural moss green, warm off-white and stone palette, tactile documentary atmosphere, sophisticated Scandinavian magazine photography, generous negative space in the upper left, no text, no logos, no badges, no dramatic mountains, no close-up faces.

## 2. Familieside – Explorer 1–3

**Format:** 16:9, bredt banner

> Quiet Nordic editorial landscape for a family outdoor learning programme. Three subtle visual moments along one safe woodland path: a map opened on a tree stump, adult and child hands pointing at a compass, distant small family group continuing along the path. Scandinavian decididuous forest, soft daylight, grounded and calm, practical learning rather than adventure tourism, no identifiable faces, no text, no logos, no survival drama, spacious composition for web overlay copy.

## 3. Skill-kurser – navigation

**Format:** 3:2

> High-end Scandinavian editorial still life about practical navigation: folded topographic map, classic compass, pencil, rain jacket fabric and a small daypack on a rock beside a quiet Danish forest trail. Natural muted greens, warm grey stone, soft side light, authentic tactile detail, no people, no text, no brand marks, no military or survival aesthetic.

## 4. Ekspeditionsture – forberedelse, ikke præstation

**Format:** 3:2

> Nordic editorial outdoor scene suggesting thoughtful preparation for a longer trip: a lone unidentifiable back-facing adult with a modest backpack pauses at a coastal trail viewpoint and studies a map, calm weather, safe visible path, Danish or Swedish landscape, understated confidence, muted forest green and natural stone palette, no dramatic cliff, no extreme sports, no logo, no text, no close-up face.

## 5. Om UdeKlar – metode og dømmekraft

**Format:** 4:3

> Scandinavian editorial image about practical learning and good judgement outdoors: a simple route sketch, compass and pencil on a bench beside a sheltered forest clearing, soft rain on leaves, quiet contemplative mood, tactile natural materials, premium Danish outdoor education brand, no people, no text, no logos, no badges.
